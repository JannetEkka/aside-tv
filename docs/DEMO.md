# Demo video: shot list

Target: 2:45 or less (judges may stop at 3:00), English, nobody on camera. Every shot is the TV screen, recorded off the Fire TV Stick (see [SETUP.md](SETUP.md), part 3). Claude edits the takes, adds titles and the narration, and makes the final MP4.

Before recording: wake the server (open its address on your phone), open Aside on the TV, and on the home screen set **Audio description: Off**, **Read answers aloud: On**, **Captions: Off**.

Each take can be long and messy. Pauses and retries are fine, because they get cut.

| # | What to do on the TV | Roughly | Why it's in the video |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home screen. Move between **Sintel** and **Tears of Steel** with the D-pad, back to Sintel, press **Play**. | 15 s | The app on a real Fire TV, driven by the remote |
| 2 | Let Sintel play for a few seconds, then hold **Fast-forward** (or press Right repeatedly) until about **05:30**. Let it play to about **05:50** (the big dragon takes Scales). Press **Up**, choose **What just happened?**, wait for the answer and let it be read out. Then select **Rewatch** on one of the timestamps. | 45 s | The core moment |
| 3 | Let it play to about **06:40**. Press **Up** → **What did I miss?** → **I've been gone 2 min**. Wait for the answer. | 30 s | The household moment |
| 4 | Press **Up** → **Ask by phone**. Scan the QR code with your phone, tap the mic and say: **"Does she ever find Scales?"** Wait for the answer on the TV. | 40 s | Spoken question from a phone, and no spoiler |
| 5 | Skip to about **09:00** (in the cave). On the phone, ask: **"Is the big dragon Scales?"** Wait for the answer. | 30 s | The hardest spoiler test, at the worst moment |
| 6 | **Back** to the home screen. Turn **Audio description: On**. Open **Sintel**, **Start over**, and let the first 60 seconds play. You'll hear the narration in the quiet stretches. | 60 s | Accessibility |
| 7 | Open **Tears of Steel**. At about **01:40** press **Up** → **Who's that?** | 30 s | A second film, live action, names held back until the film says them |

Then, separately, on the phone: a 10-second screen recording of the phone page with an answer in its list (the phone's own screen recorder).

## Narration draft (Claude records it with a synthetic voice; you can re-voice it if you like)

1. You step out for two minutes. When you come back, what did you miss? Aside is a friend on the couch who has seen exactly what you have, and never spoils what comes next.
2. Press Up on the Fire TV remote, any time. What just happened? Aside answers from the film so far, out loud, with buttons that jump back to the moment it means.
3. Back from the kitchen? What did I miss gives you exactly those two minutes.
4. Or ask anything from your phone. "Does she ever find Scales?" We'll find out together.
5. Even inside the cave, at the worst possible moment, it holds the twist. A second model checks every answer against the rest of the film, and rewrites or holds back anything that would give it away.
6. For viewers who can't see the screen, Aside writes audio description from the video itself and narrates it only when nobody is speaking.
7. It works on any film it's given. Gemini watches each one once, and every fact is stamped with the second it appears. Aside: X-Ray you can talk to.
