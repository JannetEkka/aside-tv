# Demo video: shot list

Target: 2:45 or less (judges may stop at 3:00), English, nobody on camera. Every shot is the TV screen. On a Vega OS stick, screen-capture tools don't work, so **film the TV with your phone**: phone on a stand or a pile of books, landscape, TV filling the frame, room lights down, phone volume up so it records the TV's sound. Claude edits the takes, adds titles and the narration, and makes the final MP4.

Before recording: wake the server (open its address on your phone), open Aside on the TV, and on the home screen set **Audio description: Off**, **Read answers aloud: On**, **Captions: Off**.

## Quickest: one continuous take (about 4 minutes)

Film the TV with your phone in one go, following these steps. Pauses and fumbles are fine; Claude cuts them out and adds the titles, explanation and narration.

1. **Home screen.** Move to *Tears of Steel* and back to *Sintel* with the D-pad. Press **Play**.
2. Hold **Right** (or Fast-forward) to skip to about **05:30**. Let it play to about **05:50** (the big dragon takes Scales).
3. Press **Up** → **What just happened?** Wait until the answer has been read out.
4. Press **Back**. Let the film play about 10 seconds.
5. Press **Up** → **What did I miss?** → **I've been gone 2 min**. Wait for the answer.
6. Press **Back**, then **Up** → **Ask by phone**. Scan the QR code with your phone (keep the camera on the TV; it's fine if the phone is out of shot) and ask out loud: **"Does she ever find Scales?"** Wait for the answer.
7. Press **Back**. Skip ahead to about **09:00** (inside the cave). Ask on the phone: **"Is the big dragon Scales?"** Wait for the answer.
8. Press **Back** twice to the home screen. Turn **Audio description: On**. Open *Sintel* → **Start over**, and let the first 40 seconds play.

Then upload the video to Google Drive, set sharing to **Anyone with the link**, and send Claude the link.

## Narration draft (Claude records it with a synthetic voice; you can re-voice it if you like)

1. You step out for two minutes. When you come back, what did you miss? Aside is a friend on the couch who has seen exactly what you have, and never spoils what comes next.
2. Press Up on the Fire TV remote, any time. What just happened? Aside answers from the film so far, out loud, with buttons that jump back to the moment it means.
3. Back from the kitchen? What did I miss gives you exactly those two minutes.
4. Or ask anything from your phone. "Does she ever find Scales?" We'll find out together.
5. Even inside the cave, at the worst possible moment, it holds the twist. A second model checks every answer against the rest of the film, and rewrites or holds back anything that would give it away.
6. For viewers who can't see the screen, Aside writes audio description from the video itself and narrates it only when nobody is speaking.
7. It works on any film it's given. Gemini watches each one once, and every fact is stamped with the second it appears. Aside: X-Ray you can talk to.
